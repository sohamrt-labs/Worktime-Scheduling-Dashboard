_Last updated 26 October 2026_

## 1. What we collect

Your username, email address and password (stored only as a salted one-way hash, never in readable form); the business data you enter (locations, projects, work items, meetings, equipment, team head-counts); when you accepted these terms; and your IP address, which is used briefly for rate limiting and appears in normal server logs.

## 2. How we use it

To run the service, to keep it secure (for example blocking repeated failed logins), and to send account emails: address confirmation and password reset. We do not send marketing, show advertising, or sell your data.

## 3. Cookies and local storage

One essential cookie keeps you signed in (it is HttpOnly and cannot be read by page scripts). Your browser's local storage remembers which business you last opened and that you have an account. There are no analytics or advertising cookies.

## 4. Who else sees it

Our hosting provider and the email delivery provider process data on our behalf only as needed to run the service and send account emails. We do not share your data with anyone else unless the law requires it.

## 5. How long we keep it

Until you delete your account. Deleting it removes your account and all your business data immediately; copies may remain in backups until those backups expire.

## 6. Security

Connections use HTTPS, passwords are hashed, each business's data is kept separate, and sign-in attempts are rate limited. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

## 7. Your rights

You can see and correct your data in the app, download it from Account → Download my data, and delete it from Account → Delete my account. For anything else, or to complain, contact us below; you may also contact your local data-protection authority.

## 8. Children

The service is not intended for people under 16.

## 9. Changes

We may update this policy and will show the date of the latest version at the top.

## Contact

Contact the operator of this service, using the address shown in the app.
