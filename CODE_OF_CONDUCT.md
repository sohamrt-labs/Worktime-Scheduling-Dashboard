# Code of Conduct

Everyone is welcome here. Please be respectful, patient and constructive.

**Expected:** be kind, assume good intent, give specific and helpful feedback, accept feedback gracefully, and credit other people's work.

**Not acceptable:** harassment, insults, discrimination, personal attacks, sharing other people's private information, spam, or deliberately wasting maintainers' time.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and may block people who repeatedly do. To report a problem, contact the maintainer through a private GitHub message or the contact shown on the maintainer's profile.

This applies in all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews.
